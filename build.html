The "T-shaped" strategy

Go broad on 2-3 foundational skills (the top of the T) and deep on one specialty (the stem). The Skill Explorer's hedge meter shows you which broad skills cover the most ground. Start there, then pick one to go deep on. That combination is what makes you hard to replace.

Pivoting? You're not starting over.

Every skill you've built carries forward. A nurse who learns data analysis becomes a healthcare data analyst. A teacher who learns UX becomes an instructional designer. The trick is finding where your existing skills overlap with your new direction.

Use the Skill Explorer to compare your current field with your target. You'll be surprised how many skills transfer.
