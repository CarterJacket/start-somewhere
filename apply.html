The application process, step by step

Applying isn't one thing. It's a sequence. Here's the order that works, with the best tool for each step.

1

Analyze the job posting

Before you write anything, understand what they're actually asking for. Use the Job Analyzer to extract the skills and keywords from the posting.

2

Optimize your resume for ATS

Most resumes get filtered by software before a human ever sees them. Use Jobscan to check your resume against the job description and improve your match rate.

3

Practice your interview

The best interviewees aren't naturally smooth. They've practiced. Use AI mock interviews to rehearse before the real thing.

4

Research the company

Look up the company on Glassdoor for interview questions, salary data, and employee reviews. Go in informed.

The STAR method (your interview cheat code)

For behavioral questions ("Tell me about a time when..."), structure every answer as: Situation (set the scene), Task (what you were responsible for), Action (what you specifically did), Result (what happened, ideally with numbers). Practice 5 or 6 STAR stories and you can adapt them to almost any behavioral question.
