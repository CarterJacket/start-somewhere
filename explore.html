Answer some questions, get real insights. These tools find patterns in your interests, personality, and strengths that you might not notice on your own.

60 questions that map your interests to real career clusters. Backed by the largest occupational database in the world. This is the gold standard.

Holland Code Career Test

Truity

Based on the RIASEC model. Maps you to one of six personality types (Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional) and shows matching careers.

Take the test → Assessment

16Personalities (MBTI-style)

NERIS Analytics

Not a career tool directly, but really useful for understanding how you process information, make decisions, and work with others.

Discover your type → Free

CareerOneStop Interest Assessment

U.S. Department of Labor

Quick 30-question version of the Interest Profiler. Get matched to career clusters in under 10 minutes. Great if you want fast results.

Quick assessment →

Pro tip: take more than one

No single assessment captures the full picture. Take two or three and look for overlapping themes. If "Investigative" shows up on the Holland Code and you keep landing on analytical roles in O*NET, that's a signal worth paying attention to.
