What nobody tells you

Action creates motivation.
Not the other way around.

You're waiting to feel ready. To find your passion. To know for sure. But that certainty you're looking for? It only comes after you start, not before. The people with fulfilling careers didn't have it figured out. They just moved first.

The readiness trap

You think you need to decide before you begin. So you take quizzes, read articles, ask advice. Nothing feels certain. That's not a failure of research. You just can't know what you like until you've tried it.

There are no wrong skills

That "irrelevant" course you're eyeing? Every skill you build changes how you think. It gives you an angle nobody else in the room has. The most interesting people in any field got there by combining things that didn't obviously go together.

Pivoting is the feature

Changing directions isn't quitting. It's iterating. Every pivot carries forward everything you've already learned. You're not starting over. You're starting from a better spot than last time.

Proof it works

Electric vehicles → Case competition winner.
Nobody saw that coming.

The "irrelevant" interest

A curiosity about electric vehicles. Not for a career. Not for a course. Just genuine fascination. Charging hours, battery swapping, infrastructure challenges. The kind of deep knowledge you only build when you're following interest instead of a plan.

The career path

Meanwhile, the actual career direction was consulting. The two worlds seemed totally disconnected. Nobody would put "EV nerd" and "business consultant" on the same resume. On paper, all that time spent learning about electric vehicles looked like a detour.

The collision

Then the first round of the CPABC case competition landed: plan TransLink's transition from diesel buses to electric vehicles. Suddenly, all that "irrelevant" knowledge (charging logistics, battery swapping economics, infrastructure constraints) became the exact edge no one else in the competition had.

The result

Contributions nobody else could make. Insights nobody else had. Stuff you can't just Google in a weekend.

🏆  Went on to win the finals of that case competition. All because of a "waste of time."
